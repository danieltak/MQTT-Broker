MQTT Broker bundles the following third-party components:

- Qt for Python (PySide6, shiboken6) and Qt 6 - LGPL-3.0
  https://www.qt.io/qt-for-python  -  source: https://code.qt.io/
  The Qt libraries are shipped as separate files in this package and may be
  replaced with compatible versions built from source.
- amqtt - MIT - https://github.com/Yakifo/amqtt
- psutil - BSD-3-Clause - https://github.com/giampaolo/psutil
- PyYAML, transitions, typer, dacite, pydantic, rich, pwdlib, argon2-cffi - MIT
- websockets - BSD-3-Clause
- Python runtime - PSF-2.0 - https://www.python.org/

Full license texts are available from each project's repository.
